Pokemon

A Pokemon Pure Red Nuzlocke run that chat takes part in. A Nuzlocke is a challenge players set themselves: one catch per area, and any Pokemon that faints is gone for good. Here chat decides the catch, names it after a viewer, and spends bits to send trainers against the streamer.

The Nuzlocke rules

  • One catch per routeOnly one Pokemon from each area joins the team.
  • Faint means goneNo revives, with fainting from poison outside of battle being the only exception.
  • Every catch gets a nicknameSo each loss is a named one.

What chat adds

  • Chat picks the catchNot the first one met: chat votes on which one.
  • Chat names itAfter one voter, drawn by votes.
  • Chat sends trainersBits, tips and subs send a trainer to fight the streamer.

Back to Pallet Town when

  • The whole team faintsA new run starts from scratch.
  • The streamer picks the wrong starterAny starter chat didn't vote for ends the run.

Four ways for you to play

Pick my starter, it's free!

At the start of every run, type a starter's name. Most votes wins, and the streamer has to pick that one.

Only a message that is just the name counts. Picking any other starter ends the run.

Vote on my catch, it's free!

Type a Pokemon's name from the route the streamer is on. One vote per route. The streamer can only catch the one in CATCH ALLOWED.

Throw a ball at anything else and the Poke Balls vanish from the bag.

Become one of the Pokemon

1 · VoteVote for the one that gets caught. Votes for anything else don't enter the draw.
2 · The drawOne voter is picked at random. Every vote is a ticket, so boosting raises your odds.
3 · Your nameIt takes your handle in caps, letters and numbers only, up to 10: feardragon64 becomes FEARDRAGON. If it faints, your name goes to the graveyard.

Vote even more with your money, just like democracy!

Attach the name of your Pokemon with a cheer, tip, or resub message. 100 bits per vote, subs and resubs are 5, and a buck will get you a vote too!

When it's caught, one voter's name becomes its nickname. More votes, better odds.

Summon a trainer with bits, a tip or a sub!

300 bits, a $3 tip, or any sub sends a trainer named after you. Spend more for a tougher one.

Trainers wait in line and fight one at a time. The game decides when, so the streamer can't skip one.

Or do some multitrack drifting!

One message can vote and summon a trainer. Put the Pokemon's name in it and you get both.

  • Spell it like the game. A free vote is the name and nothing else: Nidoran F, Mr. Mime.
  • Under 300 bits or under $3 buys votes but no trainer.
  • New subs and gifted subs can't vote, since there's no message. They still send a trainer, named after the gifter.
  • Naming a Pokemon that isn't on this route does nothing for votes. Your bits still count toward a trainer.
  • No timer. The leader can change right up until the throw. A tie lets the streamer catch any of the tied Pokemon.
  • Timeout with bits of 300 or more sends a trainer too, at the same tiers.
  • Outleveled everyone? An insane trainer becomes the Champion.
  • After Mewtwo falls, chat votes from a ballot of 10 Pokemon we haven't caught yet, and gifted subs add votes there instead of sending trainers.
The game

Pokemon Pure Red

A fan-made upgrade of Pokemon Red. Same Kanto, same 151, same Gen 1 feel, but with better moves, new types, and more Pokemon you can catch early. Nothing from Gen 2 or later. Made by Vortyne: PureRGB v2.6.3.

All 151 in one gameAlakazam, Machamp, Golem and Gengar evolve by level. No trading needed.
Starters in the wildSquirtle, Bulbasaur and Charmander can be caught on routes. Eevee shows up on Route 1.
New types17 Pokemon lines get a new type, like Ninetales as Fire/Ghost.
Rebalanced movesUseless moves were made useful. Seven were renamed into new ones.
Stronger healingPotion heals 50 HP, Super Potion 100. Vitamins are about 3x as strong.
Mew under the truckThe old rumor is real. Missingno is still there too, and won't wreck the save.
